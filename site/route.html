Route

Route

Tips go to agencies. This page does not collect them. If someone is in danger, skip the questions and call 911.

Hard rules

  • 911 first. If someone is being hurt, a fire is burning, or a crime is happening now, call 911. Do not open a form, and do not post first.
  • No accusations. Do not name a person in public as the one who did it. A tip is not a finding, and this site is not a courtroom.
  • No stranger photos as evidence. Do not post a photo of a person, a plate, a car, or a house as proof.
  • No vigilante threads. Do not start a pile-on, a watch list, or a thread that asks strangers to follow someone.

Walk the doors in order

Answer from the top. The first yes is the door. Your answers stay in this tab. Nothing is submitted.

Answer in order. The first yes is your door. If every answer is no, the last door is Crime Stoppers.

1. Is someone being hurt, or is a crime happening now?
2. Is this a non-emergency report for Salt Lake City Police?
3. Is this a threat, or a tip you cannot place with one agency?
4. Is this a Utah County matter?
5. Is this a federal crime?

The six doors

Use them in this order when you are choosing by hand. The questions above follow the same order. HiveWatch cannot see what you type on the next site.

1. Call 911

Someone is hurt, a fire is burning, or a crime is in progress. Stay on the line and answer the dispatcher's questions. There is no form on this site that replaces the call.

Call 911

2. Salt Lake City Police online report

For a non-emergency report inside Salt Lake City. Read their page for what they accept online. If the city tells you to call, call. This is the wrong door for a crime in progress, and it is the wrong door for a city this department does not cover.

Open the Salt Lake City Police online report

https://police.slc.gov/online-report/

3. SIAC

The Utah Statewide Information and Analysis Center takes a public report. Use it for a threat, or for a tip you cannot place with one agency. It is not a substitute for 911, and it is not the ordinary Salt Lake City police report.

Open the SIAC report form

https://siac.utah.gov/report/

4. Utah County Sheriff Tip A Cop

The sheriff's tip form for Utah County. Use it when the matter belongs there and it is not an emergency.

Open Tip A Cop

https://sheriff.utahcounty.gov/tip-a-cop

5. FBI tips

The FBI's public tip page, for a federal crime. If someone is in danger now, call 911 before you fill anything out.

Open FBI tips

https://www.fbi.gov/tips

6. Utah Crime Stoppers

An anonymous local tip line. The online form may not be live. If it does not load, do not keep retrying it as your only door, and do not post the tip in public instead. Use an agency door above, or the non-emergency number that sheriff's office publishes.

Open Utah Crime Stoppers

https://www.utahcrimestoppers.org/

Davis County and Weber County

Those counties do not have a tip form on this page. For a non-emergency, use the non-emergency number printed by that sheriff's office, or send the tip through SIAC or Utah Crime Stoppers. A missing form is not a reason to start a thread.
