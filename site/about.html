About

A small static site for listening carefully along the Wasatch Front, and for sending tips to people who can act.

Who is writing this

Placeholder. Replace this bio before you treat the page as finished. The voice below is a stand-in.

I live in Utah. I write, and I make art, and I live with a chronic illness, which is part of why I want neighborhood tools that stay short and quiet. Loud apps and late-night piles of strangers guessing at a plate number are a bad fit for the way I can pay attention.

HiveWatch Utah is a sister to Prepare Utah and FaultLine Utah. Prepare Utah is the household plan. FaultLine Utah is the earthquake page. This one is the listening page for Salt Lake, Utah, Davis, and Weber counties. Same family, different job.

I am not a police officer, a dispatcher, or a spokesperson for any department. The status line on the home page is my hand-typed read of public sources. It is not an alert.

What this is

  • A free guide to public scanner feeds, a short list of named accounts, and agency tip doors for four Wasatch Front counties.
  • A reminder to confirm what you hear before you repeat it, and to send tips to an agency.
  • A sister site to Prepare Utah and FaultLine Utah.

What this is not

  • Not an official alert system, and not a substitute for 911.
  • Not affiliated with Be Ready Utah, any police department, any sheriff's office, or any government agency.
  • Not a place to post accusations, stranger photos, or vigilante threads.
  • Not written by a police department.

Privacy

The aspiration is that HiveWatch Utah collects nothing. The pages are static files. There is no account, no analytics script, and no tracking cookie set by this site. We do not ask for your location. The Route questions are never submitted.

When you leave for Broadcastify, X, a city map, or a tip form, that site's own policy applies. Your browser is also subject to whatever its maker does. This site can decline to add another tracker. It cannot control the next website.

There is no server-side app storing what you read here. If that ever changes, this section has to change first, in plain language, before any tracker is added.
