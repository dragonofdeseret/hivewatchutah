Listen

Listen

Public safety radio for four Wasatch Front counties. The audio stays on Broadcastify.

These links open public feeds for Salt Lake, Utah, Davis, and Weber counties. HiveWatch does not host, record, or clip the audio. You may hear medical calls and people in distress. You can stop. Nothing on this page rewards you for staying tuned.

If you live outside these four counties, use your own county's public feed and your local agency. Do not stretch these links past the counties named here.

How to listen

  1. Open the county you mean. Use the card below. Broadcastify may ask you to sign in before the audio starts. That account is theirs. HiveWatch never sees it.
  2. Listen for place, time, and what was requested. If you cannot put the call on a map, let it pass.
  3. Treat the transmission as unfinished. Units are sent to check alarms, noise, and "suspicious" reports that often amount to nothing.
  4. Call 911 if someone is being hurt or a crime is happening now. Do not wait for the channel to settle it, and do not post it first.
  5. Send what an agency should have. After the fact, use Route. Leave names, plates, and photos of people off public threads.

Radio is not confirmed crime

A dispatch is a request for someone to go look. It can be wrong, late, duplicated, or about a medical call. Hearing it does not make it a fact about a neighbor. Confirm before you repeat it. When you repeat it, say where you heard it and that it is unconfirmed.

A public feed, the county index, and a separate Ogden Valley feed. You are not hearing the whole shift.

  • Open the Weber County feed index https://www.broadcastify.com/listen/ctid/2805

Some Weber police talkgroups are encrypted, so law-enforcement traffic is thinner than a full scanner would suggest. Fire is more often audible.

If a link fails, the feed may be down or renamed. Do not replace it with a random stream. Fix the card when you know the right Broadcastify page.
