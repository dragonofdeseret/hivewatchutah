Know

Open a named account before you repeat anything. A follow is not an endorsement, and a post is not a verdict.

Primary accounts are the ones to check first. County accounts are sheriff and police offices on this list. Newsrooms are for published reporting, which is still not a finding. Be Ready Utah is here to cite for preparedness, not as a crime wire.

Read the profile you land on. Handles get renamed and impersonated. If a link fails, remove the row. Do not substitute a lookalike.

Salt Lake City crime map

Salt Lake City publishes a public map of reported incidents. It covers the city. It does not cover the rest of Salt Lake County, and it does not cover Davis, Utah, or Weber. A mark on the map is a report, not a suspect and not a trial.

How to read a scanner tip

A scanner tip is a fragment. Someone heard a unit dispatched, caught a description on the air, or was told the block had police. That is a reason to pay attention. It is not a finding.

Read it in four passes. First, is anyone in danger right now? If yes, stop and call 911. Second, write down only what was said: place, time, and the nature of the call. Mark anything that was guessed. Third, ask who can confirm it. An agency post, a named official account, or a later report beats a live feed. Radio is unfinished on purpose. Officers are sent to check alarms and reports that often turn out to be nothing, and encrypted channels mean you are hearing only part of the shift. Fourth, decide where it goes. If you know something an agency should have, send it through Route. Do not turn a tip into a name, a plate, or a photo of a stranger. Do not pile replies onto a person who has not been charged.
